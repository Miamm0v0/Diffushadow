## 1. J1-J2 — status: thermodynamic-limit claim RETRACTED; open research problem
- **What is wrong**: Fig. 3h,i extrapolate <sigma_i.sigma_{i+1}> at J2=0 to
  -0.40; the exact answer (Bethe/iDMRG) is -1.7726 — a 4.4x error. Physics
  requires this local observable to be N-independent to ~1.3%; the generated
  values instead decay linearly with N.
- **Diagnosis (verified)**: suppression factor lambda(N) = generated/exact is
  the SAME at r=1 and r=2 and follows lambda ~ 1 - 0.0093(N-12) (zero at
  N~117). Distance-blind uniform suppression growing with sequence length =
  transformer length-generalisation failure, not physics. Reference figure:
  `figures/FIGREF_J1J2_ANNNI_FAILURE.png` (a,b); data via
  `code/test_decoding_j1j2.py` (steps=N sequential decoding reproduces the
  manuscript values to 0.4%, so decoding is NOT the cause; test-time RoPE
  scaling does not fix it either).
- **What we tried**: undertrained (loss 0.65) and well-trained (loss 0.019)
  models BOTH give lambda ~ 0.6 at trained sizes -> training loss is not a
  proxy for generation fidelity for Heisenberg-type data.
- **Last open lever — data volume (test RUNNING)**: 15000 snapshots/coupling
  at N=12,16,20 (vs 800-3000 before), then lambda measured at the TRAINED
  sizes. Job on thk-109 GPU5, log `logs2d/j1j2_datavol.log`, markers `[dv]`.
  Read-out: if lambda -> 1 at trained sizes, Fig. 3 J1-J2 is fixable by
  regenerating data + retraining the ladder (days). If lambda stays ~0.6,
  the failure is in the objective/masking schedule — first research problem
  for the student (below).
- **The lambda gate (use everywhere from now on)**: before trusting any
  generated correlation, check lambda = generated/exact at a size with an
  exact anchor. For SU(2) models the singlet sum rule
  sum_{r!=0} <sigma_0.sigma_r> = -3 gives a reference-FREE lambda at any N.
  Pass criterion |lambda - 1| < 0.05, PLUS a structure check (S(q) peak
  position) because amplitude alone can pass while structure is frozen
  (see ANNNI). Sparse N (e.g. 12, 24, 48, 96) is sufficient — lambda is
  linear in N.

## 5. Research problem for the student (if the data-volume test fails)
Why does the masked-diffusion objective produce lambda ~ 0.6 for
Heisenberg-type (J1-J2) shadows at TRAINED sizes, at every training
intensity, when the same architecture reaches lambda ~ 1 on TFI and Rydberg?
Suspects: information content of Pauli-6 shadows for SU(2) states (only ~1/3
of pairs share a basis; no single-site signal), masking-schedule exposure
bias, entropy-guided unmasking order. The lambda gate (§1) is the instrument;
the sum rule gives the exact anchor at any N.
