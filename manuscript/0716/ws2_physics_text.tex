The $J_1$-$J_2$ chain offers a uniquely stringent benchmark: at the
Majumdar--Ghosh point $J_2/J_1 = 1/2$ the ground state is known exactly in
closed form --- a product of nearest-neighbor singlets --- for every even
$N$ \cite{majumdar1969next}, giving analytic reference values
$\langle\boldsymbol{\sigma}_i\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}_{i+1}\rangle = -3/2$
and vanishing correlations for all $r\ge 2$. Measurements generated by
Diffushadow at this point reproduce the nearest-neighbor singlet
correlation to $-1.505$ ($0.3\%$ deviation from the exact value $-3/2$),
with the correct dimerization signature and residual longer-range
correlations below $0.11$ in absolute (Pauli) units. Notably, the residual
$r\ge2$ correlations are not random: they track the interference pattern
of the particular superposition within the two-fold-degenerate
Majumdar--Ghosh doublet that the exact-diagonalization training data
happened to contain. At exactly degenerate points the sampled ``ground
state'' is ill-defined up to such superpositions, and the generative model
reproduces the supplied mixture faithfully --- a caveat for training-data
generation at degenerate points, and simultaneously direct evidence that
the learned measurement distribution follows the training state rather
than a generic smooth interpolation. Away from the degenerate point, the
generated spin-dot correlations track exact results across the
dimerization crossover associated with the fluid--dimer transition at
$J_2^{c}/J_1 \approx 0.2411$ \cite{okamoto1992fluid}; we note that
locating this Berezinskii--Kosterlitz--Thouless-type transition precisely
requires bond-resolved dimer order parameters and level-crossing analyses
beyond the site-averaged observables considered here.

For the ANNNI model we evaluate the structure factors $S_Z(\pi)$ and
$S_Z(\pi/2)$, which probe staggered and period-four modulation components
of the longitudinal correlations. At the frustration strength studied
here, $\kappa = 0.4$, the system lies below the multicritical point
$\kappa = 1/2$: the antiphase and the floating incommensurate phase of
the high-frustration regime \cite{selke1988annni, beccaria2007floating,
nagy2011exploring} are not crossed, and the scan in $g$ traverses a single
Ising-type transition from the ferromagnet to the paramagnet, with $g_c$
reduced relative to the unfrustrated chain. Within the paramagnetic phase
the ANNNI model nevertheless retains a signature of frustration: beyond
the disorder line, known exactly from the work of Peschel and Emery
\cite{peschel1981calculation}, spin correlations change from monotonic to
oscillatory exponential decay, and the relative weight of $S_Z(\pi/2)$
grows accordingly. The evolution of the two structure factors with $g$
therefore reflects, on the ferromagnetic side, the loss of long-range
staggered-free order and, on the paramagnetic side, the crossover to
modulated short-range correlations; deep in the paramagnet both structure
factors approach the uncorrelated-limit value, so their large-$g$ behavior
is dominated by this trivial saturation rather than by any ordering
tendency. Diffushadow-generated measurements reproduce both regimes,
including the redistribution of spectral weight between $q=\pi$ and
$q=\pi/2$ across the scan.

In all ANNNI experiments reported here we fix $J_1 = 1$ and
$\kappa = 0.4$, i.e.\ below the multicritical point $\kappa = 1/2$, and
scan the transverse field $g$. The number of denoising steps in
generation equals the number of masked sites (one site unmasked per
step); see ``Measurement generation'' below.
